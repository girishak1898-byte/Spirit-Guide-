# Spirit Guide V5 — Visual Bible

## Identity
**Midnight Sanctuary**: ancient stillness presented with exceptional contemporary product craft.

## Emotional qualities
Silent. Sacred. Intimate. Cinematic. Luxurious. Timeless. Grounded. Physically believable.

Gold is scarce. Jade is scarcer. Do not turn the experience orange, yellow or emerald.

## Materials
Dark carved stone, aged dark timber, antique bronze/brass, polished black stone, still water, ivory paper, dark leather, restrained foliage.

## Lighting
Deep blue-black ambient shadows, small pools of candlelight, restrained champagne shrine light, extremely subtle volumetric haze, controlled highlights.

## Typography
Two-family system:
- Refined editorial serif for emotional/spiritual statements.
- Modern sans-serif for navigation, body, UI, forms, timers and metadata.

Approximate desktop scale:
- Eyebrow: 11–13px
- UI: 13–15px
- Body: 16–18px
- Card/object title: 20–26px
- Section statement: 42–56px
- Hero: 64–82px

Use `clamp()` and art-direct typography per breakpoint.

## Geometry
Use rounded corners selectively. Cards around 20–28px only when a card is actually warranted. Large immersive scenes should not feel like a stack of rounded boxes.

## Glass
Good uses: navigation, Temple Mode ritual dock, modal overlays, small floating wisdom/detail elements. Avoid glass on every card.

## Forbidden visual patterns
No muddy brown, bright yellow buttons, excessive gold, generic purple gradients, neon, stock-spa aesthetic, cartoon Buddhism, magic fantasy effects, giant rounded SaaS cards, fake dashboards, fake counters, fake reviews, huge empty dark sections without purpose, or identical layouts repeated section after section.
