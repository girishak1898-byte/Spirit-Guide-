## Scope discipline
Work phase-by-phase. Never continue into a later phase without explicit approval. Never replace missing production assets with random stock imagery or unrelated AI imagery.

## Benchmark
Use premium Apple product pages as a **craftsmanship benchmark** for hierarchy, pacing, scroll storytelling, restraint, motion discipline and performance. Do not copy Apple branding, source code, assets, copy, or layouts.

## Motion ownership
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger: scroll choreography, pinning, parallax, cinematic transforms, masks, hero and horizontal highlight sequences.
- Motion/Framer Motion: component presence/state only.
- CSS: hover, focus, pressed, simple micro-interactions.
- Never let two systems own the same transform on the same element.

## Visual non-negotiables
- Midnight navy / blue-black foundation.
- Warm ivory typography.
- Champagne gold is scarce.
- Jade is even scarcer.
- Buddha remains visually unobstructed.
- No muddy brown, neon, bright yellow, purple SaaS gradients, cartoon spirituality, excessive glassmorphism, fake statistics or invented Buddha quotations.

## Validation rule
A phase is not complete until typecheck, lint, relevant tests, production build, responsive checks, accessibility checks and console checks pass. If an important check fails, fix and re-run before declaring completion.
